# Code of Conduct

subpool follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work and not the person, and make
it easy for newcomers to take part. Harassment, insults, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, and
publishing anyone's private information are not acceptable here. That includes other people's account details
and seat names in logs or screenshots.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer,
[@memfactorduke](https://github.com/memfactorduke), through GitHub. If it can't be discussed in public, open
a short issue that asks for a private channel, without details, or use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature. Reports are handled in confidence.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, code and issues that break this code, and may ban anyone
from the project, temporarily or for good, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
